BDR Ingest Report

Turn a list of titles into an ingest-ready BDR report using the same ingest templates as the Title Automation tool, matched to each title's category. Reuses the Title Automation service, so ingest templates stay in one place.

Mixed mode: tag each line with its category — [movie], [tv], [talent], [game], [publisher], etc. For talent, add a profession hint in braces, e.g. Michael Jordan [talent] {basketball}, to fetch the right social accounts.
